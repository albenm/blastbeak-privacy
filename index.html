Load the cannon, Captain! Captain Boom, the pirate parrot, fires eggs from his cannon: aim at towers of crates, cans, jam jars, stone and ice, let go, and watch them topple, shatter and tumble off the table. Clear every piece before your eggs run out to win the level.

What's inside

  • 1,000 levels on ten islands, from Parrot Beach and Pirate Cove to Kraken Deep and Treasure Island, with Hard and Super Hard levels.
  • Four special shots: the exploding Bomb Egg, the heavy Ice Egg, Lightning and Parrot Strike.
  • Unlimited free retries, up to three stars per level, a chest every 10 levels and daily rewards.
  • Optional weekly leagues and a Game Center leaderboard.
  • No ads and nothing to buy inside the game. Levels play offline.
  • English, Turkish, German, French, Spanish, Portuguese and Italian.
