Blast Beak: Cannon Puzzle

A cannon physics puzzle by Kasanan Games, for iPhone.

Privacy PolicyTerms of UseSupport

Join Captain Boom, the pirate parrot, and fire your cannon at towers of crates, barrels and ice. Aim, blast and smash your way through 1000 levels across ten islands. Compete in weekly leagues if you like. The game is free to play with optional in-app purchases, has no ads, needs no account and works offline.
