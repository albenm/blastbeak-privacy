Blastbeak: Cannon Puzzle

A cannon physics puzzle by Albenk Games, for iPhone.

Privacy PolicyTerms of UseSupport

Join Captain Boom, the pirate parrot, and fire your cannon at towers of crates, barrels and ice. Aim, blast and smash your way through 1000 levels across ten islands. The game has no account and works offline.
