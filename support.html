Contact

Email albenk@gmail.com. Please include your device model, OS version and the level number if you are reporting a problem. We usually reply within 2 business days.

Frequently asked questions

I lost my progress. Progress is saved only on your device. Deleting the game or clearing its data removes it, and it cannot be restored from our side.

A purchase did not arrive. Restart the game while connected to the internet; pending purchases are delivered on start. If it still does not appear, email us with the order number from your Apple or Google receipt.

How do I get a refund? Purchases are handled by the store. Request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com (iPhone/iPad) or through Google Play order history (Android).

How do I turn off sound or vibration? Open Settings (gear icon) inside the game.
