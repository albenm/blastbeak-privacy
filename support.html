Contact

Email albenk@gmail.com. Please include your iPhone model, iOS version, the level number and, for a purchase problem, the order number from your Apple receipt email. The Support button in the game's Settings opens an email with a ready subject line. We usually reply within 2 business days.

Frequently asked questions

How do I restore my purchases?

Open Settings (gear icon) and tap Restore Purchases, with your iPhone connected to the internet and signed in to the Apple Account you used to buy. All Blastbeak purchases (coin packs, the starter pack, lives and power-up bundles and the rescue pack) are consumable items. Apple does not restore consumable items once they have been delivered, so Restore Purchases only re-delivers a purchase that was paid for but did not arrive. If a purchase did not arrive, restart the game while online and try Restore Purchases; if it still does not appear, email us the order number.

How do lives work?

You have up to 3 lives. You lose a life when you fail a level. One life comes back about every 20 minutes. When you are out of lives you can wait for the timer, refill with coins, or use a lives bundle from the shop if it is offered. Unlimited-lives periods from bundles run for the time shown.

I lost my progress after reinstalling or changing phones.

Progress, coins and purchases are saved only on your device. We have no accounts or servers, so we cannot restore them if you delete the game or reset the phone. An iCloud or computer backup of the phone that includes the game restores its data. Purchased items cannot be re-downloaded because they are consumable; if you lost a purchase this way, email us your Apple receipt and we will do our best to help.

How do I use Game Center, the leaderboard and friends?

Sign in to Game Center in iOS Settings > Game Center. The game then submits the highest level you have reached to the Apple-hosted leaderboard, and you can open the leaderboard from Settings (Leaderboard button) to see rankings and your friends. Game Center is optional; the game works fully without it. If the leaderboard stays empty, check that Game Center is turned on and that you are online.

I have a promo code. Where do I enter it?

Open Settings (gear icon), tap Captain's Pass, then Redeem a code. Apple's Redeem Code sheet opens; enter the offer code there. Codes are given out by the developer to testers, press and partners and unlock the Captain's Pass (free play) on your Apple Account. You can also redeem a code in the App Store app (your profile picture, then Redeem Gift Card or Code).

I bought the Captain's Pass on another iPhone.

Open Settings, tap Restore Purchases (or Captain's Pass, then Restore purchase). The pass is tied to your Apple Account.

A purchase did not arrive or I was charged twice.

Restart the game while connected to the internet; pending purchases are delivered on start. If it still does not appear, email us with the order number. To ask for a refund, use reportaproblem.apple.com: refunds are decided by Apple.

How do I turn off music, sound or vibration?

Open Settings (gear icon) in the game and switch Music, Sound or Haptics off.

How do I delete my data?

Delete the game from your iPhone: this removes the nickname and all progress. The game has no account to delete. See the Privacy Policy for details.

Which devices are supported?

iPhone with iOS 15 or later, in portrait orientation. The game works offline; an internet connection is needed only for the shop and Game Center.
