Contact

Email albenk@gmail.com. Please include your iPhone model, iOS version and the level number. The Support button in the game's Settings opens an email with a ready subject line. We usually reply within 2 business days.

Frequently asked questions

Is the game really free?

Yes. Blastbeak has no ads and no in-app purchases, and you can retry any level as often as you like. Coins are earned only by playing: win levels to earn them (harder levels and balls left over pay more) and spend them on continues and special shots.

I ran out of balls. What now?

Spend coins to continue with extra balls, or simply try the level again: retries are free and unlimited.

I lost my progress after reinstalling or changing phones.

Progress and coins are saved only on your device. We have no accounts or servers, so we cannot restore them if you delete the game or reset the phone. An iCloud or computer backup of the phone that includes the game restores its data.

How do I use Game Center, the leaderboard and friends?

Sign in to Game Center in iOS Settings > Game Center. The game then submits the number of levels you have cleared to the Apple-hosted leaderboard, and you can open it from Settings (Leaderboard button) to see rankings and your friends. Game Center is optional; the game works fully without it. If the leaderboard stays empty, check that Game Center is turned on and that you are online.

What is "Usage data" in Settings?

If you switch it on, the game sends anonymous gameplay statistics (such as which levels are finished or failed, and whether the game crashed) to Unity Analytics, which helps us make levels fairer. It is off unless you agreed when the game asked. Switching it off stops collection and asks Unity to delete what was collected from your installation. Details are in the Privacy Policy.

How do I turn off music, sound or vibration?

Open Settings (gear icon) in the game and switch Music, Sound or Haptics off.

How do I delete my data?

Switch Usage data off in Settings, then delete the game from your iPhone: this removes the nickname and all progress. The game has no account to delete.

Which devices are supported?

iPhone with iOS 15 or later, in portrait orientation. The game works offline; an internet connection is needed only for Game Center and, if you agreed, for sending gameplay statistics.
