Short description

Blast Beak: Ocean Rescue is a physics puzzle for iPhone from independent studio Kasanan Games. Captain Boom, a pirate parrot, loads his cannon with parrot eggs: you aim, fire and topple towers of crates, jam jars, stone and ice. Bomb Eggs explode, Ice Eggs shove whole stacks, Lightning and Parrot Strike finish the job. 1,000 levels across ten islands, no ads, offline play, seven languages, and one purchase for the whole game.

About the game

Load the cannon, Captain. In Blast Beak: Ocean Rescue every shot counts: you press anywhere on the table to aim, let go to fire, and watch towers of crates, cans, jam jars, stone and ice topple, shatter and tumble off the edge. Clear every piece before your eggs run out and the level is yours.

Captain Boom, a pirate parrot who rides on the cannon, guides you through a voyage of 1,000 levels on ten islands, from Parrot Beach and Pirate Cove to Kraken Deep and Treasure Island, each with its own lighting and music. Four special shots change how you read a tower: the Bomb Egg explodes on impact, the heavy Ice Egg shoves the whole stack, Lightning strikes from the sky and Parrot Strike sends Captain Boom diving into the pile himself. Hard and Super Hard levels test your aim, and every egg you save earns stars and coins.

There is a daily calendar, three daily tasks, weekly chests, optional online leagues and a Game Center leaderboard, but nothing to buy: Blast Beak is one purchase with no ads, it plays offline, and it speaks seven languages.

Features

  • Aim-and-release cannon physics: hold anywhere on the table to aim, let go to fire.
  • Parrot-egg ammo: the Egg, the Bomb Egg (explodes on impact) and the Ice Egg (heavy, shoves the stack), plus Lightning and Parrot Strike.
  • 1,000 levels on ten island chapters, Hard and Super Hard levels, up to three stars per level.
  • Unlimited free retries; continue with 5 extra eggs for coins earned in play.
  • Daily calendar, daily tasks, weekly chests and a log of personal records.
  • Optional online leaderboards (weekly Bronze to Diamond leagues, country, world and all-time) and Game Center.
  • No ads, offline levels, haptics, separate music, sound and vibration switches.
