Summary

Blast Beak: Ocean Rescue ("Blast Beak", "the game") is an iPhone puzzle game. It is a paid app that you buy once on the App Store; it shows no advertising and the current version contains no in-app purchases (players who bought something in version 1.0.0 keep it, see In-app purchases). There is no sign-up and no login, and your progress stays on your device. Data leaves your device in only two ways, and only if you choose them:

  • Gameplay statistics (the game asks once; you can change your answer in Settings > Usage data) go to Unity Analytics so that we can see which levels are too hard and whether the game crashes.
  • Online leaderboards (only if you tap Join on the Leaderboard tab) show your pirate name, country and points to other players, using Unity Gaming Services. You can leave at any time from the Leaderboard tab, which deletes your leaderboard profile.

We never sell or share your data, never read your advertising identifier and never track you across other apps or websites. Payments are handled entirely by Apple; we never see your payment details.

Version 1.2 and later: free to play with ads and in-app purchases

From version 1.2 the game is a free download. Everything above still applies, with these additions. Until version 1.2 is released on the App Store, the version you can buy works exactly as described in the rest of this policy.

  • Advertising (Google AdMob). Version 1.2 shows ads from Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC / Google Ireland Limited: no ads in levels 1 to 20, then at most one full-screen ad between levels (never during play), and optional rewarded ads that you choose to watch for a bonus. To show, measure and limit ads and to prevent fraud, Google's software on your device sends Google the device's IP address (approximate location), device and app information, ad interactions and identifiers: the vendor identifier and, only if you allow tracking, the advertising identifier (IDFA). Google processes this data under its own Privacy Policy and explains its use at How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.
  • Your choices. In the European Economic Area, the UK, Switzerland and other regions where it is required, the game first shows Google's consent message (Google User Messaging Platform) and asks for your consent before ads are personalised; you can change your choice at any time in Settings > Ad privacy. The game then shows Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt; if you choose Ask App Not to Track, the advertising identifier is not used. Ads are still shown, but they are less relevant. You can also turn off Personalised Ads in iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Apple Advertising.
  • No Ads and Founders. Buying No Ads removes all full-screen ads. Players who bought the game while it was paid keep an ad-free game; the game checks this with Apple's signed app purchase record on your device (StoreKit AppTransaction), and nothing about it is sent to us.
  • In-app purchases. Version 1.2 offers optional coin packs, a Starter Bundle, a Fail Rescue pack and No Ads. Apple sells and processes them; the game uses Apple's StoreKit on your device to deliver what you bought and to restore No Ads (Settings > Restore Purchases). If you allowed gameplay statistics, the game also sends Unity Analytics the product, price and outcome of a purchase (bought, cancelled, failed, restored), tied to the random installation identifier, never to your Apple Account or payment details.
  • Legal basis. Personalised ads: your consent (GDPR Art. 6(1)(a); KVKK explicit consent). Non-personalised ads, fraud prevention and frequency limits: our legitimate interest in funding a free game (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f)). Delivering purchases: performance of the contract (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b)).
  • Children. The game is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly serve personalised ads to them.

Who is responsible

The developer and data controller is Kasanan Games, based in Türkiye. For gameplay statistics and online leaderboards, Unity Technologies acts as our service provider (processor), see the sections below.

Data stored only on your device

To remember where you left off, the game saves the following on your device (in its own save file and in the iOS preferences store):

  • Your pirate name (nickname, 3 to 14 characters, chosen when you first start the game). Please do not use your real name or any personal information. The name stays on your device unless you join the online leaderboards (see below).
  • Your progress: levels completed, stars, coins, special shots, items bought in version 1.0.0, leaderboard points, missions, daily bounty and calendar progress, and your settings (music, sound, haptics, your Usage data choice and whether you joined the leaderboards).
  • A random game identifier that the game generates on first launch. It is placed in the subject of the email the game prepares when you tap Support, so that we can help you; you can edit or delete it before sending.

This data is removed when you delete the game from your device. We do not back it up; if your iPhone is backed up with iCloud or to a computer, Apple's backup rules apply to the game's files like they do for any other app.

In-app purchases

You buy the game itself on the App Store. The current version contains no in-app purchases and no Unity IAP (Unity In-App Purchasing) software: it sends no purchase events, transaction IDs or receipts, to us or to Unity. Version 1.0.0 (released on 19 October 2026 as a free download) offered optional purchases (coin packs, chests, a starter pack, the Captain's Treasure and the Captain's Pass). Players who bought them keep what they bought: coins and items stay in the save on your device, and a Captain's Pass bought in version 1.0.0 stays active. To restore the Captain's Pass after you reinstall the game or on a new device, the game asks Apple's StoreKit on your device which purchases your Apple Account already owns. This check happens only between your device and Apple: nothing is sent to us or to Unity, and no purchase sheet or sign-in prompt is shown. The game, and the purchases of version 1.0.0, are sold and processed by Apple under the Apple Media Services Terms. We never receive your name, Apple Account, card or other payment details. Purchase history is kept by Apple; refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Gameplay statistics (only with your consent)

When you first play, the game asks whether it may collect gameplay statistics. If you tap "No thanks", nothing is sent and Unity's analytics service is not even started. If you agree, the game uses Unity Analytics (Unity Gaming Services, by Unity Technologies) to record:

  • Play events: when a session starts and ends, play time, which level you start, finish or fail, the attempt number, how long it took, eggs left, continues used, and the steps of the first-run guide you completed or skipped.
  • Diagnostics: average frame rate during a level, the share of slow frames, and the type and location of a software error if one occurs.
  • Technical data recorded by Unity's software: device model, operating system and app version, language, an approximate country derived from the network connection, a random installation identifier created by Unity's software, and the device's vendor identifier (IDFV, the same for all apps of one developer on your device; it is not the advertising identifier).

These statistics are tied to the random installation identifier above (which is why Apple's privacy label lists them as "Data Linked to You"), but never to your name, email, Apple Account, Game Center profile or advertising identifier, and they are not used for advertising or tracking. We look at them only in aggregate (for example: "how many players finish level 12", "how many come back the next day"). Our legal basis is your consent (GDPR Art. 6(1)(a); KVKK explicit consent). To withdraw consent, open Settings in the game and switch Usage data to Off: collection stops at once and the game asks Unity to delete the statistics already collected from your installation.

Online leaderboards (only if you join)

The Leaderboard tab offers weekly leagues and country, world and all-time rankings. Nothing is sent until you tap Join on that tab. When you join, the game uses Unity Authentication and Unity Leaderboards (Unity Gaming Services) and sends:

  • an anonymous player ID created by Unity Authentication for this installation (no email, password or Apple Account is used);
  • your pirate name;
  • your leaderboard points (weekly points and all-time total, earned by winning levels);
  • a two-letter country code taken from your iPhone's region setting (Settings > General > Language & Region), used for the country ranking and the flag next to your name.

Other players can see your pirate name, country, points, league and rank. Do not use a name that reveals who you are. Names are checked against a filter before they are sent, and other players' names that fail it are shown as "Captain". Tap another player's row to Hide this captain (hidden on your device) or Report name (opens an email to us with that player's leaderboard ID and name, and copies the same report to your device's clipboard in case Mail does not open); we review reports within 24 hours, remove offensive names and the related entries, and may ban the player from the online leaderboards. Our legal basis is your request to take part in the leaderboards (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b)) and, for keeping the boards fair and free of abuse, our legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f)).

To leave, open the Leaderboard tab and tap Leave leaderboards (below the list) and confirm: the game asks Unity to delete your leaderboard account, name and scores, signs out and stops sending. If you join again later, a new anonymous ID is created. Weekly rankings reset every Monday; all-time scores are kept until you leave. Deleting the game without leaving first does not delete the online profile; in that case email us your pirate name and we will delete it.

Where Unity processes data

Unity may process gameplay statistics and leaderboard data on servers outside your country, including in the United States, under its own data protection safeguards (such as the EU Standard Contractual Clauses).

Invite a friend

If you tap Invite, the game opens the standard iOS share sheet with a prepared message. The message can include your pirate name. Nothing is sent unless you choose an app and a recipient yourself. The message then travels through the app you picked (for example Messages), under that app's own privacy policy.

Game Center (optional)

If you are signed in to Apple Game Center on your device, the game submits the number of levels you have cleared to a Game Center leaderboard hosted by Apple, which you can open from Settings. Game Center data (your Apple Account, Game Center name, friends, scores) is processed by Apple under Apple's Privacy Policy. We never receive, see or store it. Game Center is optional: if you are not signed in, or you disable it in iOS Settings, the game works fully without it and nothing is submitted.

Reminders (optional)

The game can remind you once a day about your daily reward, at most one reminder a day and never at night (between 10:00 and 20:00 local time). Reminders are local notifications scheduled on your iPhone by the game itself: no push token is created, nothing is sent to us or to any server, and iOS asks for your permission first. You can turn them off at any time in iOS Settings > Notifications > Blast Beak.

What we do not collect

We do not collect your real name, your email address (except when you write to us), phone number, contacts, photos, camera or microphone data, precise location, health data, payment details, browsing history or advertising identifier (IDFA). The game does not show the App Tracking Transparency prompt because it does not track you. The game contains no advertising.

Third-party software

The game is built with the Unity engine. Apart from Unity Gaming Services (Analytics only with your consent; Authentication and Leaderboards only if you join), Apple's StoreKit (used only to recognise a Captain's Pass bought in version 1.0.0) and Apple's Game Center, it contains no third-party service. It does not contain Unity IAP. If a future version introduces advertising or other data collection, this policy and our App Store privacy details will be updated before that version is released.

Links and this website

The Privacy Policy, Terms of Use and Support links in the game open this website in your browser. The site is a static page hosted on GitHub Pages; it has no cookies, scripts, analytics or external fonts of ours. Like any web host, GitHub may log technical data such as your IP address when you open the page, under GitHub's privacy statement. We do not receive that data.

Children

Blast Beak is a general-audience game and is not directed to children under 13 (or under the minimum age of digital consent in your country, up to 16). If you are below that age, please ask a parent before agreeing to gameplay statistics, or joining the online leaderboards, or simply skip them; the game works fully without them. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, write to us and we will delete it.

Retention and deletion

Game data stays on your device until you delete the game; deleting Blast Beak removes the pirate name, progress and items stored on the device, and they cannot be restored afterwards (a Captain's Pass bought in version 1.0.0 is restored automatically from your Apple Account when you install the game again). Gameplay statistics are kept by Unity under its retention rules and are deleted for your installation when you switch Usage data off. Leaderboard data is deleted when you tap Leave leaderboards, or on request by email. To remove Game Center scores, use the Game Center settings in iOS or contact Apple. Emails you send us are kept only as long as needed to answer you and are deleted no later than 12 months after the conversation ends.
