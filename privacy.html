Blastbeak: Cannon Puzzle – Privacy Policy

Effective date: 15 October 2026 · Developer: Albenk Games · Contact: albenk@gmail.com

Privacy PolicyTerms of UseSupport

Summary

Blastbeak does not collect personal data. The game works offline and has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. We do not sell or share any data.

Data stored only on your device

Your game progress (levels, coins, lives, boosters and settings) and a random game identifier are saved on your device so the game remembers where you left off. This data is never sent to us and is removed when you delete the game.

In-app purchases

Purchases are processed entirely by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). We never receive your name, payment card or billing details; the store only tells the game which item was bought so it can be delivered. Apple's and Google's own privacy policies apply to that processing.

Third-party services

The game is built with the Unity engine and uses Unity In-App Purchasing only to talk to Apple's and Google's purchase systems. Unity analytics, crash reporting, performance reporting and hardware statistics are turned off. The game contains no advertising SDKs and does not access the advertising identifier (IDFA/AAID).

Children

We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your country).

Contacting us

If you email us, we use your address only to reply and delete the conversation when it is no longer needed.

Changes

If a future version starts collecting any data, this policy and the store privacy labels will be updated before that version is released.
