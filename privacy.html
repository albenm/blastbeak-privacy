Blastbeak: Cannon Puzzle – Privacy Policy

Summary

Blastbeak: Cannon Puzzle ("Blastbeak", "the game") is a single-player iPhone puzzle game. It has no account, no sign-up, no login and no server of ours. We do not collect, receive, sell or share your personal data, and the game contains no analytics, no advertising, no crash-reporting service and no tracking. Your progress stays on your device. The only online services the game talks to are Apple's: the App Store (for purchases) and, if you choose to use it, Game Center.

Who is responsible

The developer and data controller is Albenk Games, based in Türkiye. The only personal data we ever hold is what you choose to send us when you email albenk@gmail.com (see "Contacting us").

Data stored only on your device

To remember where you left off, the game saves the following on your device (in its own save file and in the iOS preferences store):

  • Your nickname (3 to 14 characters, chosen when you first start the game). We recommend that you do not use your real name or any personal information. The nickname is shown inside the game and is never uploaded to us.
  • Your progress: levels completed, stars, coins, lives, power-ups, missions, daily bounty and calendar progress, and your settings (music, sound and haptics).
  • A random game identifier that the game generates on first launch. It is not linked to your name, your Apple Account or any advertising identifier, and it is never sent to us unless you tap Support in the game and send the email it prepares (the identifier and the game version are placed in the email subject so that we can help you; you can edit or delete them before sending).
  • A local list of the purchases you made (product and date), so that the game can deliver what you bought.

This data never leaves your device through the game. It is removed when you delete the game from your device. We do not back it up; if your iPhone is backed up with iCloud or to a computer, Apple's backup rules apply to the game's files like they do for any other app.

Invite a friend

If you tap Invite, the game opens the standard iOS share sheet with a prepared message. The message can include your nickname. Nothing is sent unless you choose an app and a recipient yourself. The message then travels through the app you picked (for example Messages), under that app's own privacy policy.

Game Center (optional)

Blastbeak uses Apple Game Center for the leaderboard and friends features. If you are signed in to Game Center on your device, the game submits the highest level you have reached to a leaderboard hosted by Apple. Rankings and friends are displayed by Apple's own Game Center screens. Game Center data (your Apple Account, Game Center name, friends, scores) is processed by Apple under Apple's Privacy Policy. We never receive, see or store it. Game Center is optional: if you are not signed in, or you disable it in iOS Settings, the game works fully without it and nothing is submitted.

In-app purchases

Purchases are processed entirely by Apple through the App Store (StoreKit). We never receive your name, Apple Account details, payment card or billing information. The App Store only tells the game that a purchase was completed so that the items can be delivered on your device. Apple's terms and privacy policy apply to that processing.

Captain's Pass and offer codes

The Captain's Pass is a one-time in-app purchase that unlocks free play (unlimited lives, free power-ups and continues, every special shot). Codes that the developer gives to testers, press, partners and friends are Apple offer codes: you enter them in Apple's own "Redeem Code" sheet (Settings in the game opens it, or use the App Store). Apple processes the code. Nothing about the code or about you is sent to us.

What we do not collect

We do not collect your name, your email address (except when you write to us), phone number, contacts, photos, camera or microphone data, precise or approximate location, health data, browsing history, advertising identifier (IDFA), usage analytics, diagnostics or crash logs. The game does not show the App Tracking Transparency prompt because it does not track you.

Third-party software

The game is built with the Unity engine and uses Unity's in-app purchasing component only to talk to Apple's App Store. Unity analytics, crash reporting and performance reporting are turned off, and the game is not linked to any Unity cloud service. The game contains no advertising software. If a future version introduces advertising or any data collection, this policy and our App Store privacy details will be updated before that version is released.

Links and this website

The Privacy Policy, Terms of Use and Support links in the game open this website in your browser. The site is a static page hosted on GitHub Pages; it has no cookies, scripts, analytics or external fonts of ours. Like any web host, GitHub may log technical data such as your IP address when you open the page, under GitHub's privacy statement. We do not receive that data.

Children

Blastbeak is a general-audience game and is not directed to children under 13 (or under the minimum age of digital consent in your country, up to 16). We do not collect personal data from anyone, including children. Parents can restrict in-app purchases with Apple's Screen Time and Ask to Buy features. If you believe a child has sent us personal information by email, write to us and we will delete it.

Retention and deletion

Game data stays on your device until you delete the game. To erase it, delete Blastbeak from your iPhone; this removes the nickname and all progress, and progress cannot be restored afterwards. To remove Game Center scores, use the Game Center settings in iOS (Settings > Game Center) or contact Apple. The game has no account, so there is no account to delete. Emails you send us are kept only as long as needed to answer you and handle follow-up questions, and are deleted no later than 12 months after the conversation ends.

Your rights

Because we do not hold your game data, there is nothing on our side to access or erase beyond your emails; you control your data directly on your device as described above. Whichever law applies to you, you can write to albenk@gmail.com and we will answer within 30 days.

  • EEA, UK and Switzerland (GDPR / UK GDPR): you have the right to access, rectify, erase, restrict or object to the processing of personal data we hold about you (which is limited to your emails), the right to data portability, and the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority. Our legal basis for handling an email you send is our legitimate interest in answering you (Art. 6(1)(f)) or, where you ask about a purchase, performance of the contract (Art. 6(1)(b)).
  • Türkiye (Law No. 6698, KVKK): as data controller, we process personal data only if you email us. Under Article 11 you may ask whether your data is processed, request information, ask for correction or deletion, and object to outcomes against you, by writing to the address above.
  • California (CCPA / CPRA) and other US states: we do not sell or share personal information, we do not use it for targeted advertising and we do not collect sensitive personal information. You may ask what we hold about you (emails only) and ask us to correct or delete it, and we will not discriminate against you for asking.

We do not transfer game data. Emails you send are handled through our email provider and may be stored outside your country.

Changes

If we change this policy, for example because a future version adds features that handle data, we will update this page and its effective date before that version is released. If a version introduces advertising, this policy and the App Store privacy information will be updated before release.

Contacting us

Albenk Games, albenk@gmail.com. If you email us we use your address and message only to reply.
