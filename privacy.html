Summary

Blastbeak: Cannon Puzzle ("Blastbeak", "the game") is an iPhone puzzle game. It is free to download, shows no advertising and offers optional in-app purchases. There is no sign-up and no login, and your progress stays on your device. Two features send data off your device, and only if you choose them:

  • Gameplay statistics (the game asks once; you can change your answer in Settings > Usage data) go to Unity Analytics so that we can see which levels are too hard and whether the game crashes.
  • Online leaderboards (only if you tap Join on the Leaderboard tab) show your pirate name, country and points to other players, using Unity Gaming Services. You can leave at any time from the Leaderboard tab, which deletes your leaderboard profile.

We never sell or share your data, never read your advertising identifier and never track you across other apps or websites. Payments are handled entirely by Apple; we never see your payment details.

Who is responsible

The developer and data controller is Kasanan Games, based in Türkiye. For gameplay statistics and online leaderboards, Unity Technologies acts as our service provider (processor), see the sections below.

Data stored only on your device

To remember where you left off, the game saves the following on your device (in its own save file and in the iOS preferences store):

  • Your pirate name (nickname, 3 to 14 characters, chosen when you first start the game). Please do not use your real name or any personal information. The name stays on your device unless you join the online leaderboards (see below).
  • Your progress: levels completed, stars, coins, special shots, items you bought, leaderboard points, missions, daily bounty and calendar progress, and your settings (music, sound, haptics, your Usage data choice and whether you joined the leaderboards).
  • A random game identifier that the game generates on first launch. It is placed in the subject of the email the game prepares when you tap Support, so that we can help you; you can edit or delete it before sending.

This data is removed when you delete the game from your device. We do not back it up; if your iPhone is backed up with iCloud or to a computer, Apple's backup rules apply to the game's files like they do for any other app.

In-app purchases

The game offers optional purchases (coin packs, chests, the Captain's Treasure and the Captain's Pass). They are sold and processed by Apple under the Apple Media Services Terms. We never receive your name, Apple Account, card or other payment details. When you buy something, Apple's StoreKit tells the game which product was bought and gives it a transaction record, which the game uses on your device to deliver the item and, for the Captain's Pass, to restore it with Restore Purchases. Purchase history is kept by Apple; refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com. If you agreed to gameplay statistics, the product bought, its price and currency are also recorded as a gameplay statistic (see the next section).

Gameplay statistics (only with your consent)

When you first play, the game asks whether it may collect gameplay statistics. If you tap "No thanks", nothing is sent and Unity's analytics service is not even started. If you agree, the game uses Unity Analytics (Unity Gaming Services, by Unity Technologies) to record:

  • Play events: when a session starts and ends, play time, which level you start, finish or fail, the attempt number, how long it took, balls left, continues used, and the steps of the first-run guide you completed or skipped.
  • Purchases: which product you bought, its price and currency (never payment details).
  • Diagnostics: average frame rate during a level, the share of slow frames, and the type and location of a software error if one occurs.
  • Technical data recorded by Unity's software: device model, operating system and app version, language, an approximate country derived from the network connection, and a random installation identifier created by Unity's software.

These statistics are tied to the random installation identifier above (which is why Apple's privacy label lists them as "Data Linked to You"), but never to your name, email, Apple Account, Game Center profile or advertising identifier, and they are not used for advertising or tracking. We look at them only in aggregate (for example: "how many players finish level 12", "how many come back the next day"). Our legal basis is your consent (GDPR Art. 6(1)(a); KVKK explicit consent). To withdraw consent, open Settings in the game and switch Usage data to Off: collection stops at once and the game asks Unity to delete the statistics already collected from your installation.

Online leaderboards (only if you join)

The Leaderboard tab offers weekly leagues and country, world and all-time rankings. Nothing is sent until you tap Join on that tab. When you join, the game uses Unity Authentication and Unity Leaderboards (Unity Gaming Services) and sends:

  • an anonymous player ID created by Unity Authentication for this installation (no email, password or Apple Account is used);
  • your pirate name;
  • your leaderboard points (weekly points and all-time total, earned by winning levels);
  • a two-letter country code taken from your iPhone's region setting (Settings > General > Language & Region), used for the country ranking and the flag next to your name.

Other players can see your pirate name, country, points, league and rank. Do not use a name that reveals who you are. Names are checked against a filter before they are sent. Tap another player's row to Hide this captain (hidden on your device) or Report name (opens an email to us with that player's leaderboard ID and name); we remove offensive names and the related entries, normally within 24 hours of a report. Our legal basis is your request to take part in the leaderboards (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b)) and, for keeping the boards fair and free of abuse, our legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f)).

To leave, open the Leaderboard tab and tap Leave leaderboards (below the list) and confirm: the game asks Unity to delete your leaderboard account, name and scores, signs out and stops sending. If you join again later, a new anonymous ID is created. Weekly rankings reset every Monday; all-time scores are kept until you leave. Deleting the game without leaving first does not delete the online profile; in that case email us your pirate name and we will delete it.

Where Unity processes data

Unity may process gameplay statistics and leaderboard data on servers outside your country, including in the United States, under its own data protection safeguards (such as the EU Standard Contractual Clauses).

Invite a friend

If you tap Invite, the game opens the standard iOS share sheet with a prepared message. The message can include your pirate name. Nothing is sent unless you choose an app and a recipient yourself. The message then travels through the app you picked (for example Messages), under that app's own privacy policy.

Game Center (optional)

If you are signed in to Apple Game Center on your device, the game submits the number of levels you have cleared to a Game Center leaderboard hosted by Apple, which you can open from Settings. Game Center data (your Apple Account, Game Center name, friends, scores) is processed by Apple under Apple's Privacy Policy. We never receive, see or store it. Game Center is optional: if you are not signed in, or you disable it in iOS Settings, the game works fully without it and nothing is submitted.

What we do not collect

We do not collect your real name, your email address (except when you write to us), phone number, contacts, photos, camera or microphone data, precise location, health data, payment details, browsing history or advertising identifier (IDFA). The game does not show the App Tracking Transparency prompt because it does not track you. The game contains no advertising.

Third-party software

The game is built with the Unity engine. Apart from Unity Gaming Services (Analytics only with your consent; Authentication and Leaderboards only if you join), Apple's StoreKit for purchases and Apple's Game Center, it contains no third-party service. If a future version introduces advertising or other data collection, this policy and our App Store privacy details will be updated before that version is released.

Links and this website

The Privacy Policy, Terms of Use and Support links in the game open this website in your browser. The site is a static page hosted on GitHub Pages; it has no cookies, scripts, analytics or external fonts of ours. Like any web host, GitHub may log technical data such as your IP address when you open the page, under GitHub's privacy statement. We do not receive that data.

Children

Blastbeak is a general-audience game and is not directed to children under 13 (or under the minimum age of digital consent in your country, up to 16). If you are below that age, please ask a parent before agreeing to gameplay statistics, joining the online leaderboards or making a purchase, or simply skip them; the game works fully without them. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, write to us and we will delete it.

Retention and deletion

Game data stays on your device until you delete the game; deleting Blastbeak removes the pirate name, progress and items stored on the device, and they cannot be restored afterwards (the Captain's Pass can be restored with Restore Purchases). Gameplay statistics are kept by Unity under its retention rules and are deleted for your installation when you switch Usage data off. Leaderboard data is deleted when you tap Leave leaderboards, or on request by email. To remove Game Center scores, use the Game Center settings in iOS or contact Apple. Emails you send us are kept only as long as needed to answer you and are deleted no later than 12 months after the conversation ends.

Your rights

Whichever law applies to you, you can write to albenk@gmail.com and we will answer within 30 days. Because the game has no account tied to your identity, the quickest ways to erase your data are in the game: Settings > Usage data Off for statistics, and Leaderboard tab > Leave leaderboards for the online rankings.

  • EEA, UK and Switzerland (GDPR / UK GDPR): you have the right to access, rectify, erase, restrict or object to processing, the right to data portability, the right to withdraw consent at any time (without affecting earlier processing), and the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority. Legal bases: consent for statistics (Art. 6(1)(a)); your request to use the leaderboards (Art. 6(1)(b)); our legitimate interests in keeping the boards fair and in replying to emails (Art. 6(1)(f)).
  • Türkiye (Law No. 6698, KVKK): statistics are processed only with your explicit consent, and leaderboard data only because you chose to join; both can be stopped in the game (Settings > Usage data, Leaderboard tab > Leave leaderboards). Under Article 11 you may ask whether your data is processed, request information, ask for correction or deletion, and object to outcomes against you, by writing to the address above.
  • California (CCPA / CPRA) and other US states: we do not sell or share personal information, we do not use it for targeted advertising and we do not collect sensitive personal information. You may ask us to access, correct or delete it, and we will not discriminate against you for asking.
